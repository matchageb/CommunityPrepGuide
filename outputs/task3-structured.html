Ready to carry

Build a household emergency kit

Pack essentials in a waterproof, easy-to-carry bag. Keep it accessible, tailor it to your family, and check it regularly.

01 / WATER & FOOD

Safe basics

  • Drinking water and ready-to-eat, non-perishable food
  • Manual can opener and reusable utensils
  • Water and food for each household member, including pets
02 / HEALTH & HYGIENE

Personal needs

  • First-aid supplies and personal medicines
  • Prescription details, eyeglasses, and masks
  • Soap, alcohol-based hand rub, and basic sanitation items
03 / LIGHT & COMMUNICATION

Stay connected

  • Flashlight, spare batteries, and a battery or hand-crank radio
  • Charged power bank, charging cable, and whistle
  • Written emergency contacts and a local area map
04 / DOCUMENTS & COMFORT

Ready for your family

  • Copies of IDs and important records in a waterproof pouch
  • Small bills, a change of clothes, and basic personal items
  • Supplies for infants, older adults, disabilities, and pets

Keep the bag light enough to carry. Follow your local DRRMO’s advice for the hazards in your area.

If you need to leave

Evacuate early.
Move safely.

Know your route and evacuation centre before an emergency. During an event, follow instructions from local authorities.

  1. 01

    Follow official directions

    Listen to your barangay or city/municipal DRRMO. Leave promptly when advised or ordered.

  2. 02

    Take your go-bag and use known routes

    Go to the designated evacuation centre. Do not walk or drive through floodwater or cross swollen rivers.

  3. 03

    Secure your home only if it is safe

    If time and conditions allow, switch off utilities as instructed, lock up, and tell someone where you are going.

  4. 04

    Wait for the all-clear

    Keep your household together at the centre. Do not return until local authorities say it is safe.
