A good plan fits
your people.

Every household is different. Think about the needs, routines, and strengths that make yours unique.

A

Plan for everyone

Include children, older adults, service animals, pets, and anyone who may need extra support.

B

Know your local risks

Learn which hazards are most likely where you live and the safest routes to shelter or higher ground.

C

Practice and refresh

Walk through your plan together, replace expired supplies, and update contacts when things change.
